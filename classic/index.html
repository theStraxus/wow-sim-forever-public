WoW: Forever simulator

Unofficial simulators and race-by-spec benchmarks for World of Warcraft: Forever beta data at level 60. Pick a simulator below for a summary, then follow its links to the full results, or open a fight length to see every spec at once.

Before you read the numbers
  • The gap between the best and worst race in a spec is usually only a few percent. Treat the results as tiers, not a strict ranking.
  • † Undead’s results lean on one racial, Touch of the Grave, which nobody has confirmed in game.
  • Gear is generated, not best-in-slot, and each race’s loadout was searched for a 5-minute fight and reused for the short ones.
  • Healing simulators are not available yet.
