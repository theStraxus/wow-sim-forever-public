Fight length: 45 s 60 s 75 s 5 min sustained Written breakdown of the short fights

Level 60 · 45-second single target · 5,000 iterations per race/build · full role-specific Tier 1 bonuses. Short fights reward cooldown racials more than a long fight does.

Read this first. The gap between the best and worst race in a spec is usually only a few percent, so treat the results as tiers, not a strict ranking. Undead’s lead in several specs comes from one racial, Touch of the Grave, which nobody has confirmed in game. The per-race loadouts were searched for a 5-minute fight and are reused unchanged here, so they may not be the best choice for 45 seconds. The written breakdown lists every caveat and a sensitivity check.
